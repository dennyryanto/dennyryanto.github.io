Portfolio redesign · Signature visuals

Six heroes, one visual language.

Each section hero gets one signature visual. Today those six visuals come from six different worlds. This board takes stock, proposes one language taken from the Garmin cockpit, and shows it on all six using the real screens.

1 · What we have

Six stages, four ways of looking, six accents, five kinds of frame.

Each visual is strong on its own. Side by side they read as six portfolios: a navy dashboard next to an ink chart, a white phone next to a black iPad, a warm lifestyle photo next to cold blue glass.

2 · The language

Instrument glass: borrow the cockpit’s camera, keep each product’s own colors.

The cockpit is the most finished piece on the site, so it sets the rules. The frame becomes the system; each brand stays inside its screen.

One stage

Every visual sits on the page ink with the cockpit’s soft radial glow. No cards and no borders around the stage.

5 · Decisions

What to settle before building it into the hi-fi.

  1. A or B?

    A (instrument glass) ties every hero to the cockpit and gives depth. B (flat specimen) is calmer and easier to keep consistent, but loses the cockpit’s character. I recommend A.

  2. Brand color inside screens only?

    Keeping LinkAja red and Dash yellow inside their screens shows real shipped work. The alternative is tinting screens toward ink, which looks tidier but less real.

  3. Photos out of the heroes?

    The e-ink nightstand photo is lovely, but it is the only photograph among six screens. It moves into the drawer.

  4. Still panes, or fans that move?

    The panes here are still. The same arrangement can become the timed fan we planned for Garmin; that waits until every section’s visuals are at the cockpit’s level.
