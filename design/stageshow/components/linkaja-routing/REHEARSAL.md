# Rehearsal: LinkAja intent routing in chat (inline card)

Redone on 2026-11-14 from screenshots of the live support chat. Beats: greeting by name and the inline four-option card build in · a cursor taps "Keluhan mengenai transaksi" ·
the tap is the user's message · its intent is classified · the reply types, then grows · the case lands in the agent queue and its attachments tick on · the agent joins ·
a leader ties the agent's line to the case (held 2.6s) · reverse-stagger exit; the stamp, composer and queue stay. Loop about 15s. The poster is the finished chat.

Captured: wide (598x470) and compact (358x450) in the harness, three frames of the loop in the built page at 1280, no console errors. Android frame, Mulish.
