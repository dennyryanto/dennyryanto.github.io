# Rehearsal: LinkAja home screen (live app)

Redrawn on 2026-11-14 from a screenshot of the live Android app; replaces the earlier carousel version. A still: no script, no cursor.
Gold: `phone-touch` for the phone. Android frame, true points (412 wide), Mulish. Layout measured in screenshot px (`--k`).

Captured: the compact design at 358 (the wide design is below its threshold, so it always draws compact), in the harness and in the built page at 1280 and 390; no console errors.
Not run: the film gate (nothing moves), reduced motion (the same still).

Assumptions: icons, logo, header buttons, banner and promo strip are crops of the screenshot; the rest is drawn. The street header art is the old drawing (the screenshot's has the status bar and logo baked in).
Unresolved: only one promo card is real, so the carousel is not animated; the Easycash mark in the balance card is a redraw; the QRIS mark is from Wikimedia Commons.
Not checked: Indonesian text at a larger system font size.
