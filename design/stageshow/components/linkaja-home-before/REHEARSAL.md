# Rehearsal: LinkAja home screen before the revamp (poster only)

A still redrawn from low-resolution screenshots of the pre-revamp app ("Linka Jadid" screen), built only so its poster can be compared with `linkaja-home` in the page's before/after
scroll-reveal. No script, no cursor, not in the site bundle. Same stage (358x736 compact, Android frame) as `linkaja-home`, so the two posters overlay exactly.

Captured: the compact design in the harness and as a poster; the reveal in the built page at 1440x900, and the stacked fallback at 390 and with reduced motion.
Assumptions: the layout and strings follow the screenshots; the icon drawings are an interpretation (Lucide glyphs at mixed weights and colours). The banner art is a placeholder gradient.
Not run: the film gate (nothing moves).
