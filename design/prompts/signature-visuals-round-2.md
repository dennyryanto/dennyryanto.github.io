## 1. What defines the style master: match all of it

- **Terrain.**
  - A 30° isometric "terrain" of stacked rectilinear blocks and stepped tiers
    sitting on a gridded base plate.
  - Channels are cut into the blocks as double-line grooves.
  - The flow always runs from **high at the back-left** to **low at the
    front-right**.
- **Measurement furniture.**
  - On the left, a vertical dimension line with a tick scale.
  - 4–6 unlabelled leader lines rising from the top edges.
  - Long dimension lines with arrowheads running parallel to the base edges.
- **Tone.**
  - Fine vertical hatching on the shaded side faces only; top faces stay
    empty.
- **Lime.**
  - Exactly one lime element, drawn as a **filled channel or face** (not a
    line), at the **end** of the flow or on the one decisive part.
  - It covers 5% of the image area or less. The master uses 0.3%.
- **Colour and line.** Same as the master: cream primary lines, grey
  construction lines, flat `#161511` background, and the same line weights.
- **Density and scale.**
  - The block module (the size of one grid cell) should match the master.
  - The drawing should span about 60–65% of the canvas width and the full
    usable height, at the same visual density as the master.

## 2. Fixes that apply to every image, including a final master

1. Deliver at **2560 × 1097**, natively or upscaled with crisp lines and no
   blur or haloing. Please also deliver the LinkAja master at that size.
2. Keep a clear **8% margin top and bottom**. In the master the top leader
   lines come within 5% of the edge.
3. **Mobile crop.** For each image, give the **x-centre (as a % of width)** of
   a 4:5 crop window that contains the lime element and the main mass of the
   drawing. The lime element must sit fully inside that window. For
   reference, the master's crop centre is about **73%**.
