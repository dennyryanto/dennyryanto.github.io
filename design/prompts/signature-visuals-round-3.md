## 1. Dash · Lumio: re-render (it breaks the family)

The idea, a central spine through a field of stations, is right. The
execution doesn't match the set:
- It fills the whole frame edge to edge. Pull it in: keep the brief's **8%
  margins** and leave as much empty space as the other images (the drawing
  spans about 60–70% of the width).
- The camera is higher and flatter than the others. Use the **same 30°
  isometric camera** as the master, with the flow running from high at the
  back-left to low at the front-right.
- The lime is a thin line crossing half the image. Make the spine a
  **filled lime channel** cut into the field, like LinkAja's outflow. It
  should end at the front-right, and the whole lime element must fit inside
  one 4:5 crop window.
- Fewer, larger stations: about 20–30 blocks at the master's block size,
  not a dense city. Show scale with a few stations trailing off toward the
  margin, not by filling the frame.

## 2. E-ink: lighter lime

The lime plate is 2.4% of the image, ten times heavier than the others
(0.25–0.7%). Keep the concept, but make the lime **a thin plate or its top
face only, with the plate smaller**, so the lime covers about 0.4–0.7%. The
rest of the drawing stays as is.

## 3. Cockpit: stronger lime

The lime pointer is 0.03% of the image and disappears at hero size. Make the
**pointer about three to five times larger**, as a clear pin or wedge
standing on the ridge at the current position, so the lime lands near
0.2–0.4%. Everything else stays.
