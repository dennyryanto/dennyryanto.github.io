Display type options

Eight Google Fonts alternatives to Clash Grotesk, each shown on the real hero, section heading and key numbers of the site, for the large headings only. Switzer stays for body text and labels.

Option 0

Clash Grotesk (current)

Sharp geometric grotesque with a slightly squared, confident feel. What the site uses today.

Trade-off. The baseline. Good, but it is common among portfolio sites and has only a hint of personality at large sizes.

Option A

Bricolage Grotesque

Quirky grotesque with ink traps and uneven rhythm, the closest step from Clash.

Trade-off. At opsz 96 the quirks are strongest; it is friendlier and a little less serious than Clash, and the narrow glyphs set tight.

Option B

Funnel Display

Clean, slightly rounded display grotesque with cut-in joins; calm and modern.

Trade-off. Safest step away from Clash: quieter, with less character at a glance, and the cut-in details only show at big sizes.

Option C

Unbounded

Wide and rounded, loud and techy.

Trade-off. It is very wide, so headings must be set about 30% smaller to keep the same line count; the big numbers feel heavy and the look is trend-dated.

Option D

Syne

Art-gallery grotesque; at 800 the letters turn extra wide and strange.

Trade-off. Only the heaviest weights are expressive, and they run wide, so it is set about 30% smaller than Clash and still breaks to three lines. Reads as creative-studio rather than leadership.

Option E

Big Shoulders

Condensed poster face, tall and dense, Chicago signage.

Trade-off. Reads as poster or sport. Very space-efficient, so it is set larger than Clash (about +14% here, not +30%, which would wrap "outcomes" to a third line), but it is the furthest from the calm Swiss tone.

Option F

Archivo expanded

Wide, sturdy grotesque in the expanded width; confident and industrial.

Trade-off. Wide, so set about 30% smaller. Solid and legible, but heavy-handed next to light body text.

Option G

Instrument Serif

High-contrast condensed serif, editorial; with "outcomes" in italic.

Trade-off. One weight only (400), so key numbers and the wordmark cannot be bolder. A strong editorial turn, but a change of register for a Swiss grid.

Option H

Fraunces

Soft, high-contrast old-style serif with warmth, at its display optical size.

Trade-off. Warm and literary rather than precise; at opsz 144 hairlines get thin on a dark ground, so weight 600 and tight tracking are needed.
