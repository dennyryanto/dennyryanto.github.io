Portfolio redesign · Signature visuals, alternatives

One case, four ways to draw it.

The stepped terraces read as one family, and a familiar one. Here is LinkAja drawn as the current terrace and as three other forms. All four use the same renderer, line weights, ink and single lime outcome, and the same one-time lime reveal when the drawing comes into view. Pick a direction and I’ll carry it across all six.

Now · Stepped terracefor comparison

A landscape that drains to one outcome.

The current form: tiers, grooves and one lime outflow into the chat screen.

Strong

Reads as process and descent; already built for all six.

Weak

Every case becomes the same landscape; the shape says little about the product.

Across six

Only the terrain varies, which is why they blur together.

A · Exploded layersRecommended

The product, pulled apart.

The interface itself floats in layers, from what it replaced to what it became: a sheet of support tickets at the bottom, a routing layer that converges, and the self-service chat on top. One lime thread climbs to the answer.

Strong

Instantly unlike a landscape. It shows real interface, which is what a design portfolio sells, and stays light: a few slabs, cheap to draw.

Weak

Exploded UI stacks are a known trope; it needs each case’s own layers to avoid feeling generic.

Across six

Komunal: shared system → three team layers → one funnel. Dash · Lumio: floor plan → orders → signage. Independent: raw data → model → decision card. E-ink: data → 1-bit render → panel. Cockpit: terrain → instruments → “you, now”.

B · Circuit boardexplore

Intakes as chips, one lime trace out.

A flat board: four intake chips route their traces through one router and leave as one lime trace into a connector that lights on arrival.

Strong

Precise and systemic; fits the “AI-native, systems” positioning and the Independent case especially.

Weak

Flat and technical, it reads as engineering rather than design, and at hero size its detail turns to texture.

Across six

Same board, different chip layouts. Distinct by topology only, so it risks the sameness we are leaving behind.

C · Floor planstrong runner-up

The journey as rooms.

A cutaway plan: four intake rooms with queued tickets, a hall of agent desks (how it used to work), and one lime route out through the door to self-service.

Strong

Tells the before/after story in one picture and gives every case a real place: the restaurant floor, the bank branch, the studio, the bedroom, the cockpit.

Weak

Walls hide paths, so the lime route must be routed carefully; denser to draw and slower than the layers.

Across six

Very distinct per case, because each place differs. Most illustrative of the four; least abstract.

Recommendation

A · Exploded layers. It breaks the family resemblance most clearly, puts each product’s own interface at the centre (the part you liked in the last round), and is the cheapest to draw. C · Floor plan is the strong alternative if you want each case to feel like a place rather than a screen.

These are first sketches on one case; whichever you pick gets the same polish pass the terraces had.
