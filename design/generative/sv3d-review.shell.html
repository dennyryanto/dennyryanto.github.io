Portfolio redesign · Signature visuals, live

Six drawings, built in code, turning as you scroll.

Each section’s signature visual is drawn live on a canvas in the line-art style we chose: stepped terrain that drains from the back-left to one lime outcome at the front-right, with a piece of each product’s own interface built into the scene. Scroll, and each one turns slowly through about 20 degrees as it passes; while it’s on screen, the lime moves: flow runs down the grooves, the e-ink plate refreshes, the cockpit pin glides.
