Portfolio redesign · Signature visuals · Exploded layers, iteration 1

Each layer makes a decision. Only some survive.

No rotation. When a drawing comes into view, the stack builds once from its foundation up. Each layer rises into place, its decisions light lime, and lime threads carry them up to the next layer. At the top, the survivors become the product’s own lime UI; decisions that did not make it dim to olive. The terraces stay as they are on their own page.

Layers

Built from what the product replaced (bottom) to what it became (top). Neutral cream and grey line.

Decisions

Lime on each layer, kept small below (tags, nodes, outlines) so the solid lime at the top stays the outcome.

Threads

Slanted lime lines join a decision to where it lands on the next layer. No leader lines and no labels.

Survivors

When the build finishes, dead ends dim to olive, so one lime trail runs from foundation to final UI.
