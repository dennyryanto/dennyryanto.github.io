PLACEMENT ROUTING · BEFORE AND AFTERILLUSTRATIVE

DepositoBPR's biggest leak was the funding step. Checkout locked the amount and payment method, then waited for the money. When the sender's transfer limit blocked it (a low bank default, a forgotten cap or a basic-tier account), the funds never came, and the user was locked out until customer service stepped in. The revamp checks every rail first (VA, BI-FAST, RTGS) and locks nothing until the funds land.
