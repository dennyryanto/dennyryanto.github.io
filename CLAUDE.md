# dennyryanto.github.io

Denny Ryanto's portfolio, served by GitHub Pages from `index.html` (a single-file
bundler export; see the technical notes in `design/AUDIT.md`).

## Redesign in progress

Before any design or layout work, read `design/AUDIT.md`. It holds the design
audit findings, the agreed "section hero + expand to reveal" pattern, decisions
already made in review (toggle style, kept gold-seal animation, Background
expanded by default), open questions, and why the work is on hold.

Mockups live in `design/mockups/`; tooling to unpack the bundle and rebuild the
hi-fi mockup lives in `design/tools/`.
