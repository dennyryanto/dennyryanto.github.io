PLACEMENT ROUTING · BEFORE AND AFTERILLUSTRATIVE

DepositoBPR's biggest leak was the funding step. Checkout locked the amount and payment method, then waited for the money. When the sender's transfer limit blocked it (a low bank default, a forgotten cap or a basic-tier account), the funds never came, and the user was locked out until customer service stepped in. The revamp checks every rail first (VA, BI-FAST, RTGS) and locks nothing until the funds land.

A self-hosted service pulls my live Garmin biometrics on a schedule and renders them as a monochrome image for a physical 7.5″ e-ink panel on my nightstand, a glanceable athlete brief that holds its image with zero power and is readable in daylight. The device runs a TRMNL DIY kit (ESP32); everything behind it I designed and built with Claude Code.

ARCHITECTURE · DATA PIPELINE
  1. 01

    Garmin Connect API

    Live biometrics, races, sleep & readiness via an unofficial Python client.

  2. 02

    FastAPI fetcher + SQLite cache

    Scheduled pulls, each endpoint wrapped in try/except; snapshots cached so a Garmin outage never blanks the display.

  3. 03

    Pillow renderer

    Draws an 800×480 canvas, then converts to 1-bit with Floyd–Steinberg dithering. Rendered on fetch, not on request.

  4. 04

    TRMNL BYOS → ESP32 e-ink

    Device polls /api/display, downloads the pre-rendered BMP and blasts it to the panel over the home LAN.

LIVE DEPLOYMENT · GCP
RUNNING

  • Compute Engine VM · Dockerized

    Ubuntu 24.04, Docker Compose. Rebuilds keep the SQLite + OAuth token volume intact.

  • APScheduler cron

    Refreshes 04:00–22:00 WIB every 30 min; overnight the device sleeps and stretches its poll interval to save battery.

  • Self-healing & alerting

    A 5-min heartbeat watches the device; if it goes silent past threshold, an outage is logged and an email alert fires via SMTP.

PERSONAL CONCEPT · GARMIN CONNECT

What if Garmin Connect read like a cockpit?

The screens
The question

Endurance training keeps me inside the Garmin ecosystem. Every morning the watch hands me a wall of instruments: readiness, Body Battery, HRV, sleep, training load. Reading it all feels like sitting in a cockpit.

So the question became: what if the app read like a well-set instrument panel? Dark display glass, fine graduated scales, one blue pointer that always means you, now.
